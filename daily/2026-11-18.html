Ugh... It's mushy! Can't I have just the meat and rice?

Vegetables help you grow strong like your favorite superhero!

채소는 네가 좋아하는 슈퍼히어로처럼 튼튼히 자라도록 도와준단다!

~를 위해 한 입만 먹어볼까? ('try one bite for...')

🎯 핵심 포인트

  • 💡 Everyone sees things differently
  • 💡 Let's find a win-win solution
